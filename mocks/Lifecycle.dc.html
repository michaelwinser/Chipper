Making things, and changing what they are

A goal is made the same three ways anything else is — in place, from capture, or out of the Pile. And nothing is stuck at the level you first gave it: a task that turns out to be an outcome climbs, a goal that turns out to be a step descends, and it keeps its title, notes, deadline, star and children either way.
